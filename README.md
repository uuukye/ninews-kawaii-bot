# ninews-kawaii-bot

You will meet ninew's kawaii bot. 

" heyo! im ninew-chan, a bot made by @ninew, and he is so mean to me :(
anyways i have three main functions:

-1: just chat with me (i learn from your responses btw)
-2: hangman word guessing game
-3: bulls and cows (number guessing game)

oh and btw if you want to delete all of your text in the keyboard, type: '/delete_all'

to continue, say /play(num), 'like /play1' :D "


The hangman and the bulls and cows game are pretty straightforward. 

 I want to mention about the chat function. The bot learns from you. And you can make connections, such that if you put in (sentence) the bot will answer (sentence).
